    Users and roles

    Who can sign in to this page and what they can do. People are disabled rather than deleted, so their name stays on the articles they wrote or reviewed.

    Admin

    Reviews every article: approve, send back, edit, pin, unpublish and delete. Adds people and changes their roles.

    Author

    Writes articles and sends them for review. Sees only their own, and can edit them until they are published.
